Our Story

Stripes, made slowly.

Rayé started in 2023 with one simple question: why is every good summer shirt either too hot, too plain, or flown in from somewhere else?

Handloom weaving workshop outside Narayanganj, Bangladesh 23°48'N 90°30'E
Where we’re from

Woven an hour from Dhaka.

Our shirts are woven, cut and sewn in a family workshop outside Narayanganj that has made cloth for three generations. We work with the same eleven people on every batch, and we pay above the industry wage because good shirts come from people who aren’t rushed.

The name is French for “striped”. The colours are borrowed from home: terracotta from local clay, faded teal from coastal ferries, yellow from mustard blossoms in winter.

How every Rayé stripe is made.

STEP 01

Yarn

Cotton and linen yarn is dyed in the stripe colours, batch by batch, before touching the loom.

STEP 02

Weave

The stripe is set on the shuttle loom one thread at a time. 48 threads of color, 48 of cream.

STEP 03

Wash

Every bolt is pre-washed and tumble-dried, so the shirt never shrinks on you after purchase.

STEP 04

Cut & Sew

Double-stitched side seams, a clean back yoke, and real shell buttons fitted by hand.

🎨

Yarn-dyed

Every stripe is dyed into the yarn before it meets the loom, so it can’t fade or wash flat off the surface.

☀️

Made for the heat

Cotton-linen blend chosen for Bangladeshi summers: it breathes, dries fast, and softens with every single wash.

🏷️

Small batches

We cut in limited runs of a few hundred. Less waste, and a shirt not everyone else in town is wearing.
