Help Centre

How can we help?

How do Rayé shirts fit? +
Short sleeve shirts are relaxed and boxy, long sleeve shirts are regular, and oversized shirts are cut a full size roomy. If you’re between sizes, size down for a cleaner look.
Will my shirt shrink in the wash? +
Every bolt is pre-washed before it is cut, so the shirt is already the size it will stay. Wash cold, hang to dry, and it won’t move or shrink.
How long does delivery take? +
Lahore & Karachi take 1–2 working days; nationwide delivery across Pakistan takes 3–5 working days. Express delivery in Lahore & Karachi arrives the next day before 8 pm.
Can I pay cash on delivery (COD)? +
Yes, anywhere across Pakistan. You can also pay via JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or card at checkout.
What is your return policy? +
Unworn shirts with tags can be returned or exchanged within 14 days. Exchanges for a different size are free, and we collect from your doorstep in Lahore & Karachi.
Do limited drops come back? +
No. Each drop is one small batch of yarn, and when it sells out we move on to the next stripe.
